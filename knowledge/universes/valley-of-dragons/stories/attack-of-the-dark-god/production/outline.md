# Attack of the Dark God — Outline

Migrated from Google Drive document `Valley of Dragons 1 - outline`.

1. **Valley of Dragons** — Humans, dragons, and elves live together in harmony.
2. **Attack of the Dark God** — The Dark God invades; the Dragon Rider Knights are formed; Gawain rescues two-year-old Alaric after his village is destroyed.
3. **Alaric** — Alaric grows into an athletic young man inspired by the dragon riders and determined to protect the valley.
4. **The Decision** — At sixteen, Alaric leaves his adoptive family to seek the path toward becoming a dragon rider.
5. **River City** — He works as a porter, blacksmith apprentice, and weaponsmith apprentice before a pilgrimage connects him with Eloran.
6. **Forest City** — Alaric trains with Eryndor, learning plantsmithing and combat.
7. **Battle of Greystone Pass** — As Kael's squire, Alaric intervenes against Lich King Mortag, is badly wounded, and is knighted as the first human Dragon Rider Knight.
8. **Champion** — Alaric becomes a mentor, bonds with Vaelor, and at twenty-eight becomes a paladin, replacing Galahad.
9. **Paladin** — A vision leads Alaric to the Lady of the Lake and the Sword of the Dawn, the weapon capable of killing the Dark God.
10. **Evil's Lair** — The paladins assault Mount Doom; Alaric kills the Dark God with the Sword of the Dawn and restores peace.
