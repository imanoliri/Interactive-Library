# Weekly Interactive Library Curator Review

Review the Interactive Library as a collection, not just as a frontend application.

## Scope

- Reader-facing books and their metadata.
- Structured knowledge under `knowledge/`.
- Curator rules under `curator/`.
- Generated per-book JSON that exposes chapters, images, media, and other extractable data.

## Tasks

1. Identify material added or changed since the previous review when that information is available.
2. Find missing or inconsistent story/universe metadata.
3. Check for canon or continuity conflicts that can be established from repository evidence.
4. Identify one to three high-value editorial corrections.
5. Identify missing illustration opportunities, prioritizing important scenes with weak image coverage.
6. Identify stale or unresolved production decisions.
7. Flag real-world factual claims that deserve verification.
8. Recommend one realistic next creative or maintenance action.

Do not invent issues merely to fill sections. Distinguish findings from suggestions and canon from production ideas.
