Land of Chivalry Across the hills the herald came, With royal seal and noble name. To Bellacourt the riders rode, Where banners bright above them flowed. They learned the lance, the shield, the steed, Of knightly vows and noble deed. Through market square and chapel hall, They saw the duties binding all. When sickness spread through western land, They answered Anselm’s stern command. Through ruined halls and dangers grim, They brought Saint Armand’s Chalice to him. But beneath the feast and tourney bright, Count Bernier plotted in the night. Lord Raymond vanished, war drew near, And Bellacourt awoke to fear. For three long days the siege held fast, While Martin rode through storm and blast. Then dragons thundered from the sky, And broke the foe with flame on high. The Church that once had feared their wings, Now praised the aid the Order brings. And Charles bestowed, with grateful hand, A tower and home in Bretonnian land. So lance and dragon, crown and creed, Were joined at last through worthy deed. Not birth alone, nor boast, nor might— But honor proved makes chivalry bright.
